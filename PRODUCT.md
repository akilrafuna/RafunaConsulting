## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) with React islands, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui (radix-nova), React Bits via the shadcn registry, GSAP 3.15 and Lenis 1.3. Deployed as a static site on GitHub Pages. Custom domain rafunaconsulting.com is inferred from the previous site's canonical URL and still needs confirming.

## Users

- Owners and managers of small and medium businesses in Kosovo (trade, construction, services, IT, gastronomy) and start-ups. They need bookkeeping, VAT/TAP/TAK declarations and support when the Tax Administration (ATK) audits them.
- Diaspora owners, mostly in Germany, Switzerland and Austria, who run a business in Kosovo from abroad and need it handled remotely by email, phone and online meetings.
- Foreign companies and investors entering Kosovo who need a local accountant and tax adviser who speaks English or German.

Job: find an accountant in Prishtina they can trust, understand exactly what is covered, and book an appointment to review their financial and tax documents.

## Product Purpose

Website for Rafuna Consulting, an accounting and tax advisory firm in Prishtina, founded 2009. Success means booked appointments by phone, WhatsApp or the booking form. It also means being the source that search engines and AI assistants cite for accounting and tax questions about Prishtina and Kosovo.

## Positioning

Founder-led since 2009 by Agron Rafuna, who holds a specialization in accounting. The team includes licensed accountants, a PhD in finance and specialised accounting assistants. The firm combines compliance work (bookkeeping, tax filings, ATK audits) with long-term financial and investment advice under one roof, and serves clients abroad remotely.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services:
1. Kontabilitet (accounting): monthly and annual balances, books of accounts, cash-flow reports, bank reconciliation.
2. Këshillim financiar (financial advisory): strategic financial planning, investment analysis, risk management, resource optimisation.
3. Këshillim tatimor (tax advisory): VAT (TVSH), personal income tax (TAP) and corporate income tax (TAK) declarations, ATK audits, lawful tax planning and savings.
4. Këshillim investiv afatgjatë (long-term investment advisory): analysis of investment opportunities, long-term portfolio planning, risk and return assessment, business expansion strategy.

Constraints: static hosting only, fast on mid-range phones over mobile data. Languages: Albanian (default, at /), English (/en/) and German (/de/).
